## Behavior, failure cases, and limits

- An exporter may answer HTTP while DB collection fails. Panels must show absence as absence and use `up{job="acres-postgres"}`, `pg_up`, and `pg_exporter_last_scrape_error` to distinguish target, database, and collector failures.
- The exporter reports a point-in-time grouped count and a maximum age among rows it observes. Its synthetic state/database rows can yield zeros when no real session exists. Validate this against a live scrape; do not interpret a zero transaction-age gauge as proof that no other operational bottleneck exists.
- Long transactions can be idle and can hold locks; the transaction-age panel identifies investigation candidates, not causality. Confirm blockers through the bounded operator SQL before taking action. Termination/cancellation of a production backend remains an operator decision outside this prompt.
- No public route, customer UI, Next/Nest component, schema migration, new package, eighth alert, threshold, SLO change, or launch sign-off is included. Query duration and wait-duration instrumentation remain separate work requiring a verified collection design and privacy review.
