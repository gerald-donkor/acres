FAQ

## Common questions

01Can Felt replace ArcGIS?

Yes. Teams run their maps, analysis, dashboards, and field data collection on Felt with no Esri license. If you use ArcGIS today, Felt connects to your services and files, so you can move over all at once or one team at a time. See [how Felt compares with ArcGIS](https://felt.com/compare/arcgis-online).

02Does Felt come with data, or do I bring my own?

Both. The Global Library has ready-to-use layers, from boundaries and demographics to flood risk, roads, and buildings, and you add your own files, databases, and live links on top.

03What data can I bring?

Almost any spatial file, including Shapefiles, GeoJSON, GeoPackages, KML, File Geodatabases, and GeoTIFFs, up to 100 GB each. Upload a spreadsheet of addresses and Felt geocodes it, or connect a database or a Google Sheet that stays up to date.

04Can I share maps outside my team?

Yes. Send a link that opens without a login, invite clients to view and comment for free, or embed a map on your website.
